How to read the colours

  • Name & Price: green = the coin is worth more now than in the original 2023 sheet; red = worth less. Gravity and Vault Tech stay plain because their 2023 prices can't be fairly compared.
  • % Below ATH: a shading scale. Deep red = almost 100% below its all-time high, yellow = in between, green = close to its high.
  • Status: red dead or barely trading · blue renamed or moved to a new token · amber not sure the right coin was matched. static = not on CoinGecko, so the price doesn't update.
